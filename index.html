How SmartBin Monitoring Works

Our sensor technology transforms ordinary bins into intelligent waste management solutions.

1. Data Collection

Weather-resistant sensors installed in bins monitor fill levels and waste conditions at regular intervals.

2. Data Processing

Our proprietary system analyzes sensor data to determine fill levels, waste types, and potential issues.

3. Actionable Insights

Data is transmitted to our cloud platform, providing real-time insights via web and mobile applications.
